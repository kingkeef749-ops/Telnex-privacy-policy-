# Telnex-privacy-policy  Telnex uses your microphone to send push-to-talk audio and notifications to alert incoming transmissions. We collect minimal data like user ID, device info, and connection logs to operate the service. Audio is not stored long-term. We do not sell your data. Data is used only to run app.




TELNEX PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 01/01/2026 

Telnex (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Telnex mobile application (“App”).

⸻

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

* Username or display name
* Phone number or email (if used for account creation)
* Direct Connect (DC) number or user ID

b. Communication Data

* Voice transmissions sent through the App
* Metadata such as timestamps, sender/receiver IDs, and connection logs

Important:
Voice transmissions are processed to deliver communication between users. We do not store audio recordings permanently unless explicitly required for functionality (such as temporary delivery or buffering).

⸻

c. Device and Usage Information

* Device type, OS version
* App version and build info
* Network status and performance logs

⸻

d. Permissions
Telnex may request access to:

* Microphone (for push-to-talk communication)
* Notifications (to alert incoming transmissions)
* Network access (to send and receive data)

⸻

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data to:

* Enable real-time communication between users
* Route audio transmissions through our servers
* Improve connection quality and performance
* Maintain security and prevent abuse
* Provide support and troubleshoot issues

⸻

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share data only:

* With service providers (e.g., cloud hosting, Firebase) necessary to operate the App
* If required by law or legal process
* To protect rights, safety, or prevent fraud

⸻

4. Data Retention

* Voice transmissions are not stored long-term
* Temporary data may be retained briefly for delivery and system performance
* Account and metadata may be retained as long as your account is active

⸻

5. Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure. We collect bare minimum data. 

⸻

6. Your Choices

You can:

* Stop using the App at any time
* Disable permissions (microphone, notifications) through your device settings
* Request account deletion (if account system is implemented)

⸻

7. Children’s Privacy

Telnex is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

⸻

8. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services such as:

* Firebase (notifications, analytics, backend services)

These services have their own privacy policies.

⸻

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Updates will be posted with a revised effective date.
